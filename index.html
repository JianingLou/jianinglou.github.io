I am a fourth-year Ph.D. student at the Center on Frontiers of Computing Studies (CFCS), School of Computer Science, Peking University under the supervision of Prof. Shaofeng Jiang.
I obtained my bachelor’s degree at Turing Class, Peking University.

I am broadly interested in theoretical computer science, especially the design and analysis of algorithms. I am currently working on optimization problems in high-dimensional Euclidean spaces, with a focus on clustering.
I study efficient algorithms for these problems in a range of computational models for processing massive datasets, including dynamic, streaming, and distributed/parallel models.
